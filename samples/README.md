# Sample sketches

The app accepts PNG, JPEG, and other formats supported by Pillow. Keep five photographed mechanism sketches here for the demo and label each with its spoken or written goal.

Suggested gallery:

- `01_ramp_bell.png` — ball rolls down a ramp into a bell
- `02_lever_chain.png` — ball tips a lever before reaching the bell
- `03_spring_release.png` — spring launches the ball toward the bell
- `04_short_ramp.png` — intentionally failing ramp for the repair loop
- `05_voice_goal.png` — sketch paired with a spoken goal

These are demo assets and should be replaced with photographs made during the event. Do not add assets whose rights are unclear.
